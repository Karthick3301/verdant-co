Yellowing Leaves Diagnostic Triage

Yellow foliage is the most frequent symptom of plant stress. Distinguishing between overwatering and nutrient depletion requires checking soil moisture depth and foliage texture.

Triage Question 1: Check Soil Moisture & Leaf Texture Step 1 of 3

Touch the lower foliage. Are the yellow leaves soft and mushy, or dry and paper-like?

Seasonal Horticultural Protocol

Spring Awakening: Repotting & Feeding Window

As natural photoperiods lengthen past 12 hours, plant metabolisms break dormancy. Complete these three essential routines this month to prevent root congestion and support foliage expansion.

Repot root-bound specimens

Priority 1

Gently lift root balls out of plastic nursery vessels. If circling roots exceed 40% of root volume, upgrade pot diameter by 2 inches using an aroid potting mix (orchid bark, perlite, and worm castings).

timer 25-35 mins yard Best for Monsteras, Ficus, Philodendrons

Initiate bi-weekly liquid kelp & nitrogen feeding

Routine

Dilute organic cold-pressed Norwegian kelp extract to half the package strength. Always water substrate thoroughly with clean filtered water first to protect sensitive feeder roots from osmotic shock.

calendar_today Every 14 days opacity 1/2 dilution

Clear mineral dust & polish stomata

Hygiene

Wipe broad leaves with a damp microfiber cloth dampened in rainwater and 2 drops of organic peppermint castile soap. Maximizes photosynthetic efficiency by up to 28%.

clean_hands 15 mins check_circle Prevents mite nesting
